## Section: Direction

- **heading** — railLabel `Direction`: Vintage gadgets, botanical texture, Lagos
- **prose:** I held three references together at once: vintage gadgets, botanical texture, Lagos iconography. None of them belong together on paper. That's what keeps this from reading like generic fintech branding.
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/06-art-direction`): Art direction: Lagos iconography, botanical texture, vintage gadgetry
- **subheading:** Color, type and gradient
- **prose:** The palette, Deep Current, Bold Velocity, Neon Pulse, Clear Horizon, Friendly Glow, Ice White, runs warm without losing the contrast a financial product needs to stay usable. DM Sans carries the type because it holds up at the small sizes people actually read inside a banking app; nothing about that choice is decorative. The gradients are spectral and textured instead of flat, so the interface reads as material you can touch.
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/03-color`): Brand colors: Deep Current, Bold Velocity, Neon Pulse, Clear Horizon, Friendly Glow, Ice White
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/04-typography`): DM Sans as the primary typeface, and why
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/05-gradients`): Spectral, textured brand gradients
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/02-logotype`): The PassportMonie logotype
- **subheading:** How it should sound
- **prose:** Fast and direct. Versatile. Personable and warm. Empowering. Four words, and each one is there to stop a playful visual system from turning careless in a product where the stakes are somebody's money.
- **image alt** (`passportmonie/07-tone-of-voice`): Tone of voice: fast and direct, versatile, personable and warm, empowering

## Section: Reflection

- **heading** — railLabel `Reflection`: The brief came before the prompt
- **prose:** The references, the palette, and the tone pillars existed before I generated a single image. That order is the entire point: I'd already made the decisions, the AI only executed them. That's why the output could ship.
